Rhythm Privacy Policy

Rhythm keeps your tasks, steps, pins, settings, and catalog search on this device.

No account · No ads · No tracking

Effective date: 8 September 2026
App: Rhythm
Version: 1.0
Developer: David “Macca” McKenzie / Macca.Studio

What stays on the device

Tasks, micro-steps, Library pins, repeating keeps, settings, and catalog search live in the app sandbox. Rhythm does not create an account. Deleting the app removes local tasks from that device. We cannot restore them from a server.

Voice

Voice is optional. The microphone is on only while you ask it to listen. You can edit the words before anything is saved. Speech stays on this device when the system allows it; otherwise the OS speech service may process it. Audio is not stored by Rhythm.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple or Google. Rhythm only remembers whether Plus is unlocked on this device.

Checklist helper

If you ask for a checklist and Rhythm has no matching ritual, a short description can go over HTTPS to Rhythm’s hosted helper, which uses Google Gemini to suggest steps. No vendor key is required. Offline, Rhythm uses an on-device list instead. Capture text is not used for advertising. Rhythm does not keep a server copy of your task list.

Weather

Weather on Today is optional. On iPhone it uses Apple Weather, and Open-Meteo if Apple Weather is unavailable. On Android it uses Open-Meteo. Rhythm has no weather server. Only an approximate area is used. You can turn this off in Settings. If location is off, the weather row does not appear.

How to delete your data

Delete the app to remove local tasks from that device. Manage or delete device backups in your Apple or Google account if you also want them gone from backups. There is no Rhythm account-deletion URL because no Rhythm account is created.

Children

Rhythm does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated servers. Parents can supervise use through normal device controls.

What we do not do

We do not include analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not sell data. Rhythm Base stays free. Rhythm is a local helper, not medical care.
