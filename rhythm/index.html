Built for the day you are actually having

Rhythm is a local helper, not medical care. Add a title, tick a checklist, or keep a repeating task. Completing a step is quiet.

Pulse, then Today

Pick Survival, Steady, or Wave Rider, then start. Today holds what you asked for, when you asked — including Daily and Weekly keeps.

A searchable catalog

Everyday rituals you can add, edit, or pin. If Rhythm has no match, a hosted helper can suggest steps. No API key to paste.

Optional weather

Today can show a coarse place and condition. Turn it off in Settings. Rhythm has no weather server of its own.

Privacy-first by design

Tasks, pins, and catalog search stay on your device. We do not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

No account. No ads. No tracking.
