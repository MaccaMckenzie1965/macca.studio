Common topics

  • Privacy & local data: see our Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting your data: delete the app. There is no Rhythm account to close.
  • Weather: optional. Turn it off in Settings. Location is approximate only.
  • Checklists: catalog rituals stay on the device. A hosted helper can suggest steps only if you ask and Rhythm has no matching ritual.
  • Purchases: Plus is optional. Restore purchases in the paywall if you already paid on this store account. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
  • Not medical care: Rhythm is a local helper. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
