Built for drivers who need the real number

Driver Prophet is a local-first trip diary and profitability calculator. Add your vehicle once, then quote, log, and review — petrol or EV — without sending your earnings to a cloud account.

Personal trips

Log distance, tolls, and running cost. Share a simple split with travellers so everyone sees their share of the trip.

Business diary

Record business trips with income and full economic cost. Export PDF or CSV for the current month or the Australian financial year.

Rideshare quoting

Enter the fare, kilometres, and trip time. See profit, fuel vs ownership vs wear, and gross/net hourly rate before you accept.

Rideshare

Shifts, goals & bookings

Start and end a shift to measure hours. Track daily and weekly earnings goals. Book jobs for later, then convert them when you drive.

Insights

Trends & hourly rates

Charts for gross, net, expenses, and net per hour over the last 7 days, 30 days, and 52 weeks — for the selected vehicle, all trip types.

Vehicles

Petrol or EV, multiple cars

Fuel fill-ups or charge sessions update consumption. Live running-cost preview while you edit insurance, loan, servicing, and more.

How running cost is calculated

Every saved trip stores the full economic cost — not just fuel. A display toggle can hide ownership on estimate screens; reports and backups still use the complete figure.

Fuel or energy

From your vehicle’s consumption and the price you last paid at the pump or charger. Log a fill-up or charge session to keep that rate current.

Ownership

Insurance, registration, servicing, tyres, loan or lease, cleaning, and other weekly costs, spread over your annual kilometres.

Estimated wear

Optional purchase price unlocks a straight-line estimate of value loss over useful life. This is not tax depreciation and is not ATO or IRS method advice.

GST is shown, not advised

Prices and income are treated as GST-inclusive. GST is shown as 1/11 of the inclusive amount at your configured rate. Check with your accountant for deductions and BAS reporting.

Data

Move between devices

Export a JSON backup in Settings and import it on another phone. There is no cloud sync and no login. Uninstalling the app removes local data unless you kept a backup.

Privacy-first by design

Your vehicles, trips, and earnings stay on your device. We do not operate servers that receive your trip records.

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Driver Prophet does not require a login, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not upload backups automatically. You choose if and where a JSON backup is shared.
