What the app does

Driver Prophet helps you track trip costs and earnings for personal driving, business use, and rideshare. Running cost is fuel or energy, plus ownership (insurance, loan, and similar), plus optional estimated wear from purchase price. Saved trips always store the full running cost.

Common questions

  • Moving data between phones: Export a JSON backup from Settings, then Import backup on the other device. There is no automatic cloud sync and no account.
  • Fuel only vs All costs: This only changes what you see on estimate screens. Saved trips keep the full running cost (fuel, ownership, and estimated wear).
  • Estimated wear: A simple straight-line estimate of value loss over useful life and annual kilometres. It is not tax depreciation and not ATO or IRS method advice. Wear is $0 until you enter a purchase price.
  • GST figures: Amounts are treated as GST-inclusive. GST shown is 1/11 of the inclusive amount at your configured rate. This is not tax advice.
  • Shifts & hourly rates: Start and end a shift on the Rideshare tab to measure hours. Gross and net per hour are shown for the current shift, today, this week, and this month. Quick Quote also shows $/hr for that job when you enter trip time.
  • Trends: Charts cover gross, net, expenses, and net per hour for the selected vehicle over the last 7 days, 30 days, and 52 weeks.
  • Business export: PDF or CSV for the current calendar month or the Australian financial year (1 July – 30 June).
  • iPhone: Version 2.0 is iPhone, iOS 17+. All features in this release are included at no charge; there are no in-app purchases.
  • Android Pro: Optional Google Play subscriptions may unlock Business and Rideshare on Android. Personal trips remain available without a subscription. Billing is handled by Google Play.
  • Privacy & local data: see our Privacy Policy.
