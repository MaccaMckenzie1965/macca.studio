Driver Prophet Privacy Policy

Local-First & On-Device Architecture

Effective Date: July 9, 2026
App: Driver Prophet (com.macca.driverprofit)
Version: 2.3.0

Driver Prophet ("the App") is a trip-cost and profit-tracking tool built with a "Local-First" architecture. We believe your vehicle, shift, trip, and financial data should remain private and under your absolute control on your device.

1. Summary & No Central Server Data

Driver Prophet does not collect, store, or transmit your personal income, vehicle information, or trip records to our own servers or advertising third parties.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to sign up or log in with an email address to use the App.
  • No Trackers or Ads: We do not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or advertising networks.


2. Local Storage & Device Data

All core data you enter—including vehicle profiles, odometer readings, earnings, costs, shifts, and daily notes—is stored exclusively on your physical device in a private database.

  • We do not operate servers that collect or back up your trip records.
  • If you clear app data or uninstall the App, your local data is permanently removed unless you have exported a JSON backup yourself or enabled Android automatic device backup.


3. Internet Connections & Third-Party Services

The App connects to external internet services strictly to support the following specific features:

  • Weather (Open-Meteo): When viewing weather details, your approximate latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve a local forecast.
  • Subscriptions (Google Play Billing): Purchases and subscription statuses are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

4. Location Permissions & GPS Usage

Location permissions are requested strictly for local functionality:

  • GPS Trip & Shift Tracking: Active only while tracking is enabled. Location updates are processed locally on your phone to calculate total distance. Raw GPS coordinate trails are not saved long-term—only the final accumulated distance is kept with your trip.
  • Weather: A one-time location check is used to retrieve local weather conditions.

5. Data Export & Backups

You can export JSON backups or share business PDF/CSV reports through Android's standard share mechanisms. You control where these files are sent. If Android's system-level device backup is enabled, your app settings and local data may be backed up to your encrypted Google Account storage in accordance with Google's policies.

6. Children’s Privacy

Because we do not operate account servers or harvest personal information, Driver Prophet is not designed to collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
