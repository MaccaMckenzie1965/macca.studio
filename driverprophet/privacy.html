Driver Prophet Privacy Policy

Local-first · On-device · No accounts · No ads · No tracking

Last updated: 1 September 2026
App: Driver Prophet (com.macca.driverprofit)
Version: 2.0.0
Developer: David “Macca” McKenzie / Macca.Studio

Driver Prophet (“the App”) is a trip-cost and profit-tracking tool. We believe your vehicle, trip, and financial data should remain private and under your control on your device.

1. Summary

Driver Prophet does not collect, store, or transmit your personal income, vehicle information, or trip records to Macca.Studio servers or to advertising third parties.

  • No account required: You do not need to sign up or log in.
  • No ads or trackers: The App does not include third-party analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or advertising networks.
  • No background location tracking: The App does not request location permission and does not record GPS trails.

2. Data stored on your device

The App may store the following on your device only:

  • Vehicle details (make, model, fuel or energy use, running costs, optional purchase information)
  • Trip records (distance, earnings, expenses, duration, dates, descriptions)
  • Rideshare shift sessions (start and end times used to calculate hours worked)
  • Bookings you save for later
  • App preferences (theme, GST rate, annual kilometres, daily and weekly goals, display options)
  • Backup files you export manually (JSON)

If you clear app data or uninstall the App, this local data is permanently removed unless you exported a JSON backup yourself or restored from an operating-system device backup.

3. Internet & third-party services

Core quoting, logging, trends, and backup features work without sending your trip data to Macca.Studio.

  • Privacy Policy and Support links: Opening these in Settings loads pages on macca.studio in your device browser. That is a normal HTTPS page load, not collection of your trip records.
  • Send feedback: If you choose to email us, that uses your mail app. You control what you send.
  • Android subscriptions (optional): The Android version may offer Google Play Pro subscriptions. Purchases and subscription status are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • iOS: Version 2.0.0 does not include in-app purchases or subscriptions. All features described on the product page are included at no charge.

The App does not include weather APIs, cloud sync, or developer-operated backup servers.

4. Data export & backups

You can export a JSON backup from Settings and import it on another device. You can also share business PDF or CSV reports through the operating system’s share sheet. Those files are under your control. The App does not upload backups automatically.

If Android automatic device backup or iCloud/device backup is enabled, the operating system may include locally stored app data in encrypted backups according to Google or Apple policies — not according to a Macca.Studio cloud service.

5. How to delete your data

Because Driver Prophet does not create online accounts, you control deletion on your device:

  • Delete individual trips, bookings, vehicles, or shift records in the App.
  • Uninstall the App or clear app storage to remove remaining local data.
  • To remove data from OS backups, manage or delete device backups in your Google or Apple account settings separately.

There is no separate account-deletion URL because no Macca.Studio account is created when you use Driver Prophet.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated servers.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. The current policy is published at this URL.
