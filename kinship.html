What Kinship is

Kinship is not another social network. It is a personal system for managing the complexity of real relationships the history, the nuance, the emotional tone, and the follow-through.

Friend profiles that remember what matters

Keep notes, important context, and your own observations organised per person. Kinship helps you hold the thread, so your next message is grounded in reality.

Quick Chat coaching (draft + refine)

Generate a clean, sendable draft without the awkward meta-text. Choose tone, add your own instructions, and get a message you can actually send.

Conversation insight that builds self-awareness

Spot patterns in your communication and how you come across. Get concrete suggestions on what to do and what to avoid, without being judged.

Core

Context + memory log

Track facts, events, and interaction notes so you dom't lose momentum with the people you care about.

Coaching

Message generation + rewriting

Draft replies and rewrite tone,while keeping your voice. Built for real-life constraints: time, anxiety, and ambiguity.

Insight

Psychological frameworks

Optional, readable insight using well-known models (e.g., attachment, Big Five, ego states) to support healthier communication.

How it works

Kinship turns scattered messages and memories into a usable, private relationship workflow, without turning your life into a spreadsheet.

1) Add a person

Import from contacts or add manually. Start with the basics, then add the context you wish you always remembered.

2) Log interactions

Capture the essentials after a call, message, or catch-up. Over time, Kinship becomes a reliable memory and pattern library.

3) Use coaching when it matters

Draft a reply, rewrite tone, or analyze a tricky message. You stay in control; Kinship helps you move with clarity.

Privacy-first by design

Kinship is built to support trust. Your relationship history is sensitive, and we treat it that way.

Privacy-first, with clear AI disclosure

Kinship is designed to keep personal relationship data on-device wherever possible. AI features you enable send selected content directly to Google Gemini and/or Anthropic Claude using API keys you provide — not to Macca.Studio servers.
