Built for reverent, distraction-free prayer

Pocket Rosary is an uncluttered missal-style companion — liturgically thoughtful, fast, and respectful of your attention. Every devotion in version 2.1 works without an internet connection.

Heart-loop Rosary canvas

A heart-shaped bead path with haptic feedback. Gold light follows the cord as you advance; the current bead breathes; beads already prayed in the decade keep a faint trail. Compact phones scale the canvas so the prayer stays on screen.

Mysteries of the day

Joyful (Monday, Saturday), Sorrowful (Tuesday, Friday), Glorious (Wednesday, Sunday), Luminous (Thursday) — or the complete 20-decade Rosary. Tap a mystery header for meditations from Scripture, the Catechism, Lumen Gentium, and Munificentissimus Deus.

Traditional prayers, your way

The core sequence is the traditional Rosary. Optional toggles add an opening intention, Fatima Prayer, decade intentions, Marian invocation, Memorare, the Pope’s monthly intentions, St. Michael, and the Litany of Loreto. A prayer drawer holds the familiar texts.

Mass

Order of Mass

2010 ICEL English responses with Latin, phonetic help, newcomer notes, and posture cues (stand, sit, kneel). Congregational parts are visually distinct. Jump from the Liturgy of the Word to today’s readings. A participation aid — not a substitute for the Missal used in your parish.

Hours

Night Prayer (Compline)

The Church’s traditional bedtime prayer: opening verse, Psalms 4, 90 (91), and 133 (134), a short reading from Jeremiah, the Nunc Dimittis, and the night blessing. Psalm and canticle texts use the Douay-Rheims translation.

Readings

Today’s Mass readings

First Reading, Responsorial Psalm with antiphon, and Gospel for the Australian 2026 liturgical calendar (Year A; Advent begins Year B). Douay-Rheims texts mapped to Roman Lectionary citations — not the official published Lectionary translation.

Stay with the prayer

Settings stay out of the way until you need them. Progress, intentions, and journal totals never leave the phone.

Novena tracker

Name an intention and mark days 1 through 9. Progress is saved on the device and can be reset when the novena is finished.

Daily Rosary reminder

Opt in under Settings and choose a time. A gentle local notification names today’s mystery. Nothing is sent to Macca.Studio or any third party.

Private prayer journal

On-device totals for Rosaries finished, counts by mystery group, and current and best streaks. Visible only in Settings — never on the Home screen, never uploaded.

Display that stays with you

Dark mode, haptics, keep-screen-on while you pray, auto-save of bead position and mystery group, plus optional fruit-of-the-mystery and scriptural clauses on each decade.
