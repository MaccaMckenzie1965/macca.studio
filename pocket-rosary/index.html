Built for reverent, distraction-free prayer

Pocket Rosary is designed as an uncluttered missal-style companion — liturgically thoughtful, fast, and respectful of your attention.

Interactive Rosary canvas

Navigate beads with tactile feedback, automatic mystery selection by day of week, and rich doctrinal meditations from Scripture and the Catechism.

Complete devotional library

Rosary, Chaplet, Order of Mass, Compline, Australian daily readings, and a novena tracker — all available without an internet connection.

Configurable & persistent

Optional prayers, opening intentions, haptics, dark mode, and auto-save of bead progress across app restarts.

Privacy-first by design

Your prayer life is sacred. Pocket Rosary keeps personal intentions and progress on your device — not on our servers.

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Version 1.0 does not require login, does not include analytics SDKs, and does not transmit your prayer intentions to Macca.Studio. Optional one-time tips on iOS and Android store builds support development only — they do not unlock core prayer content.
