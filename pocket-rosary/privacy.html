Pocket Rosary Privacy Policy

Offline-first · No accounts · No ads · No tracking

Effective Date: August 17, 2026
App: Pocket Rosary
Version: 1.0.1
Developer: David “Macca” McKenzie / Macca.Studio

Pocket Rosary (“the App”) is a quiet, offline Catholic prayer companion. Prayer texts, mystery meditations, and liturgical guides are stored on your device. We do not operate user accounts, advertising networks, analytics services, or developer-controlled cloud servers for this App.

1. Summary

We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal prayer data to Macca.Studio or to third-party advertisers or analytics providers.

  • No account required: You can use the App without signing up or logging in.
  • No ads or trackers: The App does not include third-party analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising networks, or social login.
  • No network required: Core prayers work fully offline. Version 1.0.1 does not connect to Macca.Studio servers.
  • No user-generated public content: Text you enter (such as prayer intentions) stays on your device and is not published or shared with other users.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information on your device only to support your prayer experience:

  • Prayer preferences: theme (light/dark), haptics, keep-screen-on, optional Rosary prayers, and display toggles.
  • Custom opening intention: optional text you enter for your Rosary opening prayer.
  • Novena tracker: your novena intention text and day progress (1–9).
  • Rosary progress: current bead position, selected mystery group, and sequence fingerprints used to restore your place after the App closes.
  • Daily reminder settings: whether the opt-in Rosary reminder is enabled and your chosen notification time.
  • Prayer journal: on-device totals for Rosaries finished, per-mystery counts, and streaks (visible only in Settings).
  • On-device date/time: used locally to select the mystery of the day and display daily Mass readings; this is not sent to us.

Some of this information may be personally meaningful or religious in nature. It is treated as private user content and is never uploaded to Macca.Studio servers.

3. Device Backups

On Android, the App may allow the operating system to include locally stored data in encrypted device backups when Android automatic backup is enabled (android:allowBackup). Such backups are managed by Google under your Google Account and device settings—not by Macca.Studio.

On iOS, locally stored preferences may be included in encrypted iCloud or local device backups according to your Apple device settings.

We do not operate our own backup service for Pocket Rosary.

4. Internet & Third Parties

Core prayer features do not require internet access. Rosary, Mass, Compline, readings, and local preferences work entirely on your device without connecting to Macca.Studio servers.

Version 1.0.1 does not include in-app purchases, third-party analytics, advertising networks, or billing SDKs.

If you enable the optional daily Rosary reminder in Settings, the App schedules gentle local notifications on your device naming today’s mystery. Notification content is not sent to Macca.Studio or any third party.

Opening the Privacy Policy or Support links in the App uses your device’s web browser to load pages on macca.studio. That is a normal HTTPS page load, not collection of your prayer data.

5. Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you edit or clear it, reset relevant features (such as the novena tracker), clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. Data restored from an OS-level backup may reappear if you restore a device backup.

6. How to Delete Your Data

Because Pocket Rosary does not create online accounts, you control deletion entirely on your device:

  • Clear your custom opening intention in Settings.
  • Tap Reset novena on the Novena Tracker screen to clear novena intention and day progress.
  • Clear App storage (Android: Settings → Apps → Pocket Rosary → Storage → Clear data) or delete the App to remove all locally stored information.
  • To remove data from OS backups, manage or delete device backups in your Google or Apple account settings separately.

There is no separate account-deletion URL because no Macca.Studio account is created when you use Pocket Rosary.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated servers. Because all meaningful data stays on the device and no account is required, parents and guardians can supervise use through normal device controls.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes (for example, if new features or optional in-app support options are added). The effective date at the top will be revised, and the updated policy will be published at this URL.
