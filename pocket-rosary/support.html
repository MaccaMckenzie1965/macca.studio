Common topics

  • Privacy & local data: see our Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting your data: clear intentions in Settings, reset the novena, or uninstall the App — full steps are in the Privacy Policy.
  • Offline use: all core prayers work without an account or internet connection.
  • iPhone only: version 1.0.1 is designed for iPhone. iPad and Mac App Store versions are not available in this release.
  • Order of Mass: a participation aid using the 2010 ICEL English translation with Latin and newcomer notes. It is not a substitute for the official Missal used in your parish.
  • Mass readings: Douay-Rheims texts mapped to the Australian 2026 liturgical calendar. This is not the official published Lectionary translation.
  • Daily reminder: opt in under Settings. If notifications do not appear, check iOS notification permission for Pocket Rosary.
  • Prayer journal: Rosary totals and streaks stay on your device only and are visible in Settings.
  • In-app purchases: version 1.0.1 has no in-app purchases. Refunds do not apply to this version.
