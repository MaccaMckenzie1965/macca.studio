Common topics

  • Privacy & local data: see our Privacy Policy.
  • Deleting your data: clear intentions in Settings, reset the novena, or uninstall the App — full steps are in the Privacy Policy.
  • Offline use: all core prayers work without an account or internet connection.
  • Voluntary tips: optional one-time tips on iOS and Android store builds only. Tips support development and do not unlock features.

Optional tips & purchases

Pocket Rosary offers four optional consumable tips in the App Store and Google Play versions. They are a voluntary way to support ongoing development — not donations to a registered charity, and not required to use any prayer content.

  • Prices: shown in your local currency by Apple or Google before you confirm.
  • No features unlocked: tips do not remove ads (there are none), grant status, or unlock prayers.
  • Repeat tips: each tip is a consumable purchase; you may tip again if you wish.
  • Refunds: all billing is handled by Apple or Google. Macca.Studio cannot process refunds directly. Request a refund through Apple or Google Play according to their policies.
  • Purchase not completing: confirm you are signed into the store, have a valid payment method, and are using a store build (TestFlight, Play internal/closed testing, or production) — not a sideloaded APK. If a tip still fails, email us with your device, OS version, and App version.
  • Desktop: standalone macOS, Windows, and Linux builds do not include in-app store purchases.
